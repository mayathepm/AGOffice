A Word From Our Founder
While we’re preparing to scale our internal team, we’re building the infrastructure around something that was already happening.

As a project management professional working across agency and enterprise environments, I’ve built relationships with recruiters and staffing partners throughout my career.

I’m regularly contacted about contract and project opportunities. Over time, peers in my LinkedIn network started reaching out asking if I could connect them with recruiters and staffing partners I’ve worked with.

Sometimes I know the person asking well. Sometimes I don’t.

So I built a streamlined way to understand the person, vet the experience, and determine whether there’s an appropriate referral connection to make, without slowing down the submission process.

If you have the credentials and experience, the AGOffice™ Referral Pipeline gives you a structured way to submit your profile, connect with me briefly, and be considered for relevant opportunities or referral pathways.

Maya Anders
