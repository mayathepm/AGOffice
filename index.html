Governance in Practice

Different environments. Different problems. The same need for work to move with more clarity.

Building the System

A creative studio was operating through ad hoc requests and production workflows.

Intervention: Established a PMO and migrated work into a governed intake and production pipeline.
Impact: Full PMO stood up from zero.
Governing the Flow

A multi-portfolio environment needed stronger controls around how work advanced and resources were allocated.

Intervention: Overhauled workflow governance with stage gates and resourcing rules.
Impact: ~20% throughput gain.
Coordinating Complexity

Multiple retail campaigns were moving simultaneously against a shared launch date.

Intervention: Established the coordination and delivery structure needed to manage overlapping work.
Impact: 3-month engagement extended to 12 months.
Governing High-Volume Review

A regulated communications program required a reliable way to manage review and approval at scale.

Intervention: Built the governance structure across the review and approval process.
Impact: 300+ assets governed.
Bringing Structure to Migration

A technical platform migration was moving forward without a formal delivery plan.

Intervention: Audited the work and established a structured migration and delivery plan.
Impact: Migration re-planned and delivered on time.
