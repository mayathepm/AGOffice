Engagement models

Start with the need. Choose the level of support.

There are a few ways to work with us. Scope and shape are set around your work, and not every engagement follows the same path.

01

Advisory and consultation

Targeted guidance on a decision, a design or a challenge you are working through.

Typical shape: working sessions and written recommendations.
02

Assessments and defined-scope engagements

Evaluate the current state and desired outcomes before deciding what to change.

Typical shape: a fixed scope, defined deliverables and a readout.
03

Project-based implementation and optimization

Design and put in place the governance, workflows or PMO the work needs.

Typical shape: phased delivery against agreed outcomes.
04

Embedded expertise and ongoing support

A practitioner working inside your team, with support that adjusts as needs change.

Typical shape: ongoing, fractional and fully remote.

Final scope, timing and commercial terms are confirmed in conversation, after we understand the work.
